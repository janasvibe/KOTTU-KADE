The Takatak Rhythm

More than just food,
it's Sri Lankan Culture.

Kottu Roti isn't just a dish; it's the heartbeat of Sri Lankan nightlife. The iconic metal-on-metal clash of the kottu blades against the hot griddle creates a hypnotic rhythm that echoes through the streets of Colombo.

At KOTTU KADE, we honor this street food heritage. We use the same traditional methods, the same fiery spices, but elevate it with premium ingredients and our signature melting cheese to give you an unforgettable experience.
